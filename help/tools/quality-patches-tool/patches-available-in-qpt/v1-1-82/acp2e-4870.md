# ACP2E-4870: Product Alerts emails ignore store view email settings

The ACP2E-4870 patch fixes the issue where Product Alerts email notifications ignore store view email settings. This patch is available when the [[!DNL Quality Patches Tool (QPT)]](/help/tools/quality-patches-tool/quality-patches-tool-to-self-serve-quality-patches.md) 1.1.82 is installed. The patch ID is ACP2E-4870. Please note that this issue is scheduled to be fixed in Adobe Commerce 2.4.10.

## Affected products and versions

**The patch is created for Adobe Commerce version:**

* Adobe Commerce (all deployment methods) 2.4.7-p8

**Compatible with Adobe Commerce versions:**

* Adobe Commerce (all deployment methods) 2.4.5 - 2.4.9

>[!NOTE]
>
>The patch might become applicable to other versions with new [!DNL Quality Patches Tool] releases. To check if the patch is compatible with your Adobe Commerce version, update the `magento/quality-patches` package to the latest version and check the compatibility on the [[!DNL Quality Patches Tool]: Search for patches page](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/tools/commerce-quality-patches/index.html). Use the patch ID as a search keyword to locate the patch.

## Issue

Product Alerts email notifications ignore store view email settings.

<u>Steps to reproduce</u>:

1. Use a website that has at least two store views.
1. On the Admin sidebar go to **[!UICONTROL Stores]** > **[!UICONTROL Settings]** > **[!UICONTROL Configuration]** > **[!UICONTROL Catalog]** > **[!UICONTROL Catalog]**. In Product Alerts, set Allow Alert When Product Comes Back in Stock to Yes for the website scope.
1. On the Admin sidebar go to **[!UICONTROL Stores]** > **[!UICONTROL Settings]** > **[!UICONTROL Configuration]** > **[!UICONTROL Catalog]** >  **[!UICONTROL Inventory]**. In Stock Options, set Display Out of Stock Products to Yes so the out-of-stock product can be opened on the storefront.
1.  On the Admin sidebar go to **[!UICONTROL Stores]** > **[!UICONTROL Settings]** > **[!UICONTROL Configuration]** > **[!UICONTROL Advanced]** > **[!UICONTROL System]**. In Mail Sending Settings, keep Disable Email Communications set to No in the Default Config scope.
1. Change the configuration scope to the non-default store view and set Disable Email Communications to Yes.
1. Create a customer account on the non-default storefront.
1. Go to Catalog > Products and create a simple product assigned to the website. Set it to Out of Stock and make sure that it is visible on the non-default storefront.
1. Open the out-of-stock product on the non-default storefront and click Notify me when this product is in stock.
1. Update the product to In Stock.
1. Wait for the `catalog_product_alert` cron job and the related consumer process the alert.
1. Check the customer inbox.

<u>Expected results</u>:

The customer from the non-default store view does not receive a product alert email.

<u>Actual results</u>:

The product alert processing resolves the email setting from the default scope instead of the custom store view and ignores the store-scoped Disable Email Communications value.
