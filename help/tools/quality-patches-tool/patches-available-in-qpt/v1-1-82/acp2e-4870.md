# ACP2E-4870: Product alert emails ignore store view email settings

The ACP2E-4870 patch fixes the issue where product alert email notifications ignore store view email settings. This patch is available when the [[!DNL Quality Patches Tool (QPT)]](/help/tools/quality-patches-tool/quality-patches-tool-to-self-serve-quality-patches.md) 1.1.82 is installed. The patch ID is ACP2E-4870. Please note that this issue is scheduled to be fixed in Adobe Commerce 2.4.10.

## Affected products and versions

**The patch is created for Adobe Commerce version:**

* Adobe Commerce (all deployment methods) 2.4.7-p8

**Compatible with Adobe Commerce versions:**

* Adobe Commerce (all deployment methods) 2.4.5 - 2.4.9

>[!NOTE]
>
>The patch might become applicable to other versions with new [!DNL Quality Patches Tool] releases. To check if the patch is compatible with your Adobe Commerce version, update the `magento/quality-patches` package to the latest version and check the compatibility on the [[!DNL Quality Patches Tool]: Search for patches page](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/tools/commerce-quality-patches/index.html). Use the patch ID as a search keyword to locate the patch.

## Issue

Product alert email notifications ignore store view email settings.

<u>Steps to reproduce</u>:

1. Use a website with at least two store views.
1. On the Admin sidebar, go to **[!UICONTROL Stores]** > **[!UICONTROL Settings]** > **[!UICONTROL Configuration]** > **[!UICONTROL Catalog]** > **[!UICONTROL Catalog]**. In **[!UICONTROL Product Alerts]**, set **[!UICONTROL Allow Alert When Product Comes Back in Stock]** to **[!UICONTROL Yes]** for the website scope.
1. On the Admin sidebar, go to **[!UICONTROL Stores]** > **[!UICONTROL Settings]** > **[!UICONTROL Configuration]** > **[!UICONTROL Catalog]** > **[!UICONTROL Inventory]**. In **[!UICONTROL Stock Options]**, set **[!UICONTROL Display Out of Stock Products]** to **[!UICONTROL Yes]** so that the out-of-stock product can be opened on the storefront.
1. On the Admin sidebar, go to **[!UICONTROL Stores]** > **[!UICONTROL Settings]** > **[!UICONTROL Configuration]** > **[!UICONTROL Advanced]** > **[!UICONTROL System]**. In **[!UICONTROL Mail Sending Settings]**, leave **[!UICONTROL Disable Email Communications]** set to **[!UICONTROL No]** in the **[!UICONTROL Default Config]** scope.
1. Change the configuration scope to the non-default store view and set **[!UICONTROL Disable Email Communications]** to **[!UICONTROL Yes]**.
1. Create a customer account on the non-default storefront.
1. Go to **[!UICONTROL Catalog]** > **[!UICONTROL Products]** and create a simple product assigned to the website. Set the product to **[!UICONTROL Out of Stock]** and ensure that it is visible on the non-default storefront.
1. On the storefront, open the out-of-stock product on the non-default storefront and select **[!UICONTROL Notify me when this product is in stock]**.
1. Go to **[!UICONTROL Catalog]** > **[!UICONTROL Products]** and set the simple product to **[!UICONTROL In Stock]**.
1. Wait for the `catalog_product_alert` cron job and the related consumer to process the alert.
1. Check the customer inbox.

<u>Expected results</u>:

The customer on the non-default store view does not receive a product alert email.

<u>Actual results</u>:

A product alert email is sent to the customer from the non-default store view even though **[!UICONTROL Disable Email Communications]** is set to **[!UICONTROL Yes]** for that store view.
