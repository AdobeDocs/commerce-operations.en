# ACP2E-4877: Edit button missing for *Pending* [!UICONTROL Payment on Account] orders in Admin

The ACP2E-4877 patch fixes the issue where orders placed with **[!UICONTROL Payment on Account]** can't be edited in the Admin while they're in *Pending* status. This patch is available when the [[!DNL Quality Patches Tool (QPT)]](/help/tools/quality-patches-tool/quality-patches-tool-to-self-serve-quality-patches.md) 1.1.83 is installed. The patch ID is ACP2E-4877. Please note that this issue is scheduled to be fixed in Adobe Commerce B2B 1.5.4.

## Affected products and versions

**The patch is created for Adobe Commerce version:**

* Adobe Commerce (all deployment methods) B2B 1.5.2-p4

**Compatible with Adobe Commerce versions:**

* Adobe Commerce (all deployment methods) B2B >=1.3.4 <1.5.4

>[!NOTE]
>
>The patch might become applicable to other versions with new [!DNL Quality Patches Tool] releases. To check if the patch is compatible with your Adobe Commerce version, update the `magento/quality-patches` package to the latest version and check the compatibility on the [[!DNL Quality Patches Tool]: Search for patches page](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/tools/commerce-quality-patches/index.html). Use the patch ID as a search keyword to locate the patch.

## Issue

Orders placed with **[!UICONTROL Payment on Account]** can't be edited in the Admin while they're in *Pending* status.

<u>Prerequisites</u>:

Adobe Commerce with the [!DNL B2B] extension. **[!UICONTROL Enable Company]** is set to *Yes*, **[!UICONTROL Payment on Account]** and **[!UICONTROL Cash On Delivery]** are *enabled*, and the customer is assigned to a company.

<u>Steps to reproduce</u>:

1. In the Admin, go to **[!UICONTROL Sales]** > **[!UICONTROL Orders]** and click **[!UICONTROL Create New Order]**.
1. Select a customer who is assigned to a company, and add a product.
1. In the **[!UICONTROL Payment Method]** section, select **[!UICONTROL Payment on Account]**, and click **[!UICONTROL Submit Order]**.
1. Open the new order from **[!UICONTROL Sales]** > **[!UICONTROL Orders]**.
1. Repeat the steps with **[!UICONTROL Cash On Delivery]** as the payment method, and open that order.

<u>Expected results</u>:

The **[!UICONTROL Edit]** button is displayed on the order view page for both *Pending* orders, the one placed with **[!UICONTROL Payment on Account]** and the one placed with **[!UICONTROL Cash On Delivery]**.

<u>Actual results</u>:

The **[!UICONTROL Edit]** button is missing for the *Pending* order placed with **[!UICONTROL Payment on Account]**, while it's displayed for the **[!UICONTROL Cash On Delivery]** order.
