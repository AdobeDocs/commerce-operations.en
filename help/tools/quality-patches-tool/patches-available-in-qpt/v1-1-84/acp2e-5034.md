# ACP2E-5034: B2B item discounts don't reduce dynamic-price bundle subtotals in negotiable quotes

The ACP2E-5034 patch fixes the issue where negotiable quote management incorrectly resets totals to zero when recalculating a quote after selecting a shipping method, discards updates to bundle product option quantities made through the **[!UICONTROL Configure]** action in the Admin, and doesn't correctly reflect item-level discounts applied to dynamic price bundle products in quote subtotals. This patch is available when the [[!DNL Quality Patches Tool (QPT)]](/help/tools/quality-patches-tool/quality-patches-tool-to-self-serve-quality-patches.md) 1.1.84 is installed. The patch ID is ACP2E-5034. Please note that this issue is scheduled to be fixed in Adobe Commerce B2B 1.5.4.

## Affected products and versions

**The patch is created for Adobe Commerce version:**

* Adobe Commerce (all deployment methods) B2B 1.5.2-p5

**Compatible with Adobe Commerce versions:**

* Adobe Commerce (all deployment methods) B2B >= 1.5.0 < 1.5.3

>[!NOTE]
>
>The patch might become applicable to other versions with new [!DNL Quality Patches Tool] releases. To check if the patch is compatible with your Adobe Commerce version, update the `magento/quality-patches` package to the latest version and check the compatibility on the [[!DNL Quality Patches Tool]: Search for patches page](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/tools/commerce-quality-patches/index.html). Use the patch ID as a search keyword to locate the patch.

## Issue

Negotiable quote management incorrectly resets totals to zero when recalculating a quote after selecting a shipping method, discards updates to bundle product option quantities made through the **[!UICONTROL Configure]** action in the Admin, and doesn't correctly reflect item-level discounts applied to dynamic price bundle products in quote subtotals.

<u>Prerequisites</u>:

B2B is installed.

<u>Steps to reproduce</u>:

1. Set **Admin** > **[!UICONTROL Stores]** > **[!UICONTROL Settings]** > **[!UICONTROL Configuration]** > **[!UICONTROL General]** > **[!UICONTROL B2B Features]**. Set **[!UICONTROL Enable Company]** = *Yes*. Set **[!UICONTROL Enable B2B Quote]** = *Yes*.
1. In Admin, go to **[!UICONTROL Customers]** > **[!UICONTROL Companies]**, click **[!UICONTROL Add New Company]**, create an active company account, and click Save.
1. Have the company administrator set a password from the welcome email.
1. In Admin, go to **[!UICONTROL Catalog]** > **[!UICONTROL Products]**, and create at least two simple products.
1. In Admin, go to **[!UICONTROL Catalog]** > **[!UICONTROL Products]**, then choose **[!UICONTROL Bundle Product]** from the **[!UICONTROL Add Product]** menu. Set **[!UICONTROL Dynamic Price]** = *Yes*, add at least two bundle options with a different simple product in each option, and select **[!UICONTROL User Defined]** for each bundle item's quantity.
1. On the storefront, sign in as the company administrator, and add the dynamic-price bundle product to the cart at least twice with different option configurations.
1. In the cart, click **[!UICONTROL Request a Quote]**, complete the **[!UICONTROL Add your comment]** and **[!UICONTROL Quote Name]** fields, and click **[!UICONTROL Request a Quote]** to submit the quote.
1. In Admin, go to **[!UICONTROL Sales]** > **[!UICONTROL Quotes]** and, in the **[!UICONTROL Actions]** column for the quote, click **[!UICONTROL View]**.
1. In **[!UICONTROL Items Quoted]**, click **[!UICONTROL Configure]** for one bundle item, change the bundle option quantities and item quantity, and click **[!UICONTROL OK]**.
1. Click **[!UICONTROL Update Prices]**, then click **[!UICONTROL Proceed]**.
1. For the same item, open the **[!UICONTROL Action]** menu and select **[!UICONTROL Discount item]**, choose a fixed-amount discount, enter the amount, and click **[!UICONTROL Confirm]**.
1. Click **[!UICONTROL Add to Quote]**, then click **[!UICONTROL Update Prices]** and click **[!UICONTROL Proceed]**.

<u>Expected results</u>:

1. The configured dynamic-price bundle quote item retains the updated option and item quantities.
1. The fixed item-level discount is applied to the bundle item and reduces the Subtotal and Subtotal (Excluding Tax) values.

<u>Actual results</u>:

The fixed discount appears in the **[!UICONTROL Discount]** column, but it doesn't reduce the dynamic-price bundle item's Subtotal or Subtotal (Excluding Tax) values after the quantities are updated.
