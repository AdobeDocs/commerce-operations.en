# ACP2E-5127: Saving a company with a non-English Admin locale resets Credit Limit to zero

The ACP2E-5127 patch fixes the issue where saving a company in the Admin resets its [!UICONTROL Credit Limit] to `0` when the Admin user's [!UICONTROL Interface Locale] is not English. This patch is available when the [[!DNL Quality Patches Tool (QPT)]](/help/tools/quality-patches-tool/quality-patches-tool-to-self-serve-quality-patches.md) 1.1.84 is installed. The patch ID is ACP2E-5127. Please note that this issue is scheduled to be fixed in Adobe Commerce B2B 1.5.4.

## Affected products and versions

**The patch is created for Adobe Commerce version:**

* Adobe Commerce (all deployment methods) B2B 1.5.3

**Compatible with Adobe Commerce versions:**

* Adobe Commerce (all deployment methods) B2B 1.3.3-1.5.3

>[!NOTE]
>
>The patch might become applicable to other versions with new [!DNL Quality Patches Tool] releases. To check if the patch is compatible with your Adobe Commerce version, update the `magento/quality-patches` package to the latest version and check the compatibility on the [[!DNL Quality Patches Tool]: Search for patches page](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/tools/commerce-quality-patches/index.html). Use the patch ID as a search keyword to locate the patch.

## Issue

Saving a company in the Admin resets its [!UICONTROL Credit Limit] to `0` when the Admin user's [!UICONTROL Interface Locale] is not English, for example German.

<u>Prerequisites</u>:

B2B is installed.

<u>Steps to reproduce</u>:

1. In the Admin, go to **[!UICONTROL Stores]** > **[!UICONTROL Settings]** > **[!UICONTROL Configuration]** > **[!UICONTROL General]** > **[!DNL B2B] [!UICONTROL Features]** and set **[!UICONTROL Enable Company]** to *[!UICONTROL Yes]*.
1. Add a new company in the Admin. Set **[!UICONTROL Credit Limit]** to *1234.56* and click **[!UICONTROL Save]**.
1. In the Admin, go to **[!UICONTROL System]** > **[!UICONTROL Permissions]** > **[!UICONTROL All Users]**, click the Admin user's name, set **[!UICONTROL Interface Locale]** to *[!UICONTROL German (Germany)]*, and click **[!UICONTROL Save User]**. Sign in as that user.
1. Reopen the same company, change another field, such as [!UICONTROL Company Legal Name], without changing [!UICONTROL Credit Limit], and click **[!UICONTROL Save]**.
1. Reopen the company and check **[!UICONTROL Credit Limit]** in the **[!UICONTROL Company Credit]** section.

<u>Expected results</u>:

The [!UICONTROL Credit Limit] remains the same.

<u>Actual results</u>:

The [!UICONTROL Credit Limit] is reset to `0`.
