# ACP2E-5041: Saving a stale product form expires the special price after a scheduled update

The ACP2E-5041 patch fixes the issue where saving a product edit page that was opened during a scheduled update, after the update ended, sets the special price end date to the end time of the update, so the special price expires. This patch is available when the [[!DNL Quality Patches Tool (QPT)]](/help/tools/quality-patches-tool/quality-patches-tool-to-self-serve-quality-patches.md) 1.1.84 is installed. The patch ID is ACP2E-5041. Please note that this issue is scheduled to be fixed in Adobe Commerce 2.4.10.

## Affected products and versions

**The patch is created for Adobe Commerce version:**

* Adobe Commerce (all deployment methods) 2.4.7-p10

**Compatible with Adobe Commerce versions:**

* Adobe Commerce (all deployment methods) 2.4.5 - 2.4.8-p5

>[!NOTE]
>
>The patch might become applicable to other versions with new [!DNL Quality Patches Tool] releases. To check if the patch is compatible with your Adobe Commerce version, update the `magento/quality-patches` package to the latest version and check the compatibility on the [[!DNL Quality Patches Tool]: Search for patches page](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/tools/commerce-quality-patches/index.html). Use the patch ID as a search keyword to locate the patch.

## Issue

Issue where saving a product edit page that was opened during a scheduled update, after the update ended, sets the special price end date to the end time of the update, so the special price expires.

<u>Steps to reproduce</u>:

1. Open a product in the Admin. Set Price to 160. Under Price, click Advanced Pricing, set Special Price to 150, click Done, and then click Save.
1. Verify that the product displays a price of 150 on the storefront.
1. Create a new scheduled update. Set Start Date to T**2 minutes and End Date to T**5 minutes, where T is the current time in minutes. Set Special Price to 140 and save the scheduled update.
1. At T+3 minutes, verify that the product displays a price of 140 on the storefront.
1. At T+3 minutes, open the same product for editing in a new Admin tab and leave the product edit page open without saving.
1. At T+6 minutes, verify that the scheduled update has ended and the product displays a price of 150 on the storefront.
1. In the previously opened product edit tab, click Save without refreshing the page.
1. Reload the product on the storefront.

<u>Expected results</u>:

1. During the scheduled update, the storefront displays the scheduled special price of 140.
1. After the scheduled update ends, the storefront reverts to the permanent special price of 150 and remains at 150 after the previously opened product form is saved.

<u>Actual results</u>:

1. Saving the stale product form silently writes the expired scheduled update end time to the permanent Special Price To Date.
1. The storefront displays the regular price of 160 because the permanent special price of 150 is treated as expired, with no error or warning.
