# ACP2E-5041: Saving a stale product form expires the special price after a scheduled update

The ACP2E-5041 patch fixes the issue where saving a product edit page that was opened during a scheduled update sets the special price end date to the update’s end time, which causes the special price to expire. This patch is available when the [[!DNL Quality Patches Tool (QPT)]](/help/tools/quality-patches-tool/quality-patches-tool-to-self-serve-quality-patches.md) 1.1.84 is installed. The patch ID is ACP2E-5041. Please note that this issue is scheduled to be fixed in Adobe Commerce 2.4.10.

## Affected products and versions

**The patch is created for Adobe Commerce version:**

* Adobe Commerce (all deployment methods) 2.4.7-p10

**Compatible with Adobe Commerce versions:**

* Adobe Commerce (all deployment methods) 2.4.5 - 2.4.8-p5

>[!NOTE]
>
>The patch might become applicable to other versions with new [!DNL Quality Patches Tool] releases. To check if the patch is compatible with your Adobe Commerce version, update the `magento/quality-patches` package to the latest version and check the compatibility on the [[!DNL Quality Patches Tool]: Search for patches page](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/tools/commerce-quality-patches/index.html). Use the patch ID as a search keyword to locate the patch.

## Issue

Saving a product edit page that was opened during a scheduled update, but saved after the update ended, sets the special price end date to the update’s end time. As a result, the special price expires.

<u>Steps to reproduce</u>:

1. In the Admin, open a product, and then set **[!UICONTROL Price]** to *160*.
1. Under **[!UICONTROL Price]**, select **[!UICONTROL Advanced Pricing]**, set **[!UICONTROL Special Price]** to *150*, select **[!UICONTROL Done]**, and then select **[!UICONTROL Save]**.
1. On the storefront, verify that the product price is *150*.
1. Create a new scheduled update, and then set **[!UICONTROL Start Date]** to *T+2 minutes* and **[!UICONTROL End Date]** to *T+5 minutes*, where T is the current time.
1. Set **[!UICONTROL Special Price]** to *140*, and then save the scheduled update.
1. At T+3 minutes, verify that the product price on the storefront is *140*.
1. At T+3 minutes, open the same product in a new Admin tab. Leave the product edit page open and don’t save it.
1. At T+6 minutes, verify that the scheduled update has ended and that the product price on the storefront is *150*.
1. In the product edit tab you left open, select **[!UICONTROL Save]** without refreshing the page.
1. Reload the product page on the storefront.

<u>Expected results</u>:

1. During the scheduled update, the storefront displays the scheduled special price of *140*.
1. After the scheduled update ends, the storefront reverts to the permanent special price of *150* and remains at *150* after you save the previously opened product edit page.

<u>Actual results</u>:

1. Saving the previously opened product edit page silently writes the end time of the expired scheduled update to the permanent **[!UICONTROL Special Price To Date]**.
1. The storefront displays the regular price of *160* because it treats the permanent special price of *150* as expired. No error or warning appears.
