# ACP2E-5061: Category merchandising rule can't combine AND conditions with condition that matches several values

The ACP2E-5061 patch fixes the issue where a category merchandising rule cannot combine AND conditions with a condition that matches any of several values. This patch is available when the [[!DNL Quality Patches Tool (QPT)]](/help/tools/quality-patches-tool/quality-patches-tool-to-self-serve-quality-patches.md) 1.1.86 is installed. The patch ID is ACP2E-5061. Please note that this issue is scheduled to be fixed in Adobe Commerce 2.4.10.

## Affected products and versions

**The patch is created for Adobe Commerce version:**

* Adobe Commerce (all deployment methods) 2.4.7-p5

**Compatible with Adobe Commerce versions:**

* Adobe Commerce (all deployment methods) 2.4.5 - 2.4.9

>[!NOTE]
>
>The patch might become applicable to other versions with new [!DNL Quality Patches Tool] releases. To check if the patch is compatible with your Adobe Commerce version, update the `magento/quality-patches` package to the latest version and check the compatibility on the [[!DNL Quality Patches Tool]: Search for patches page](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/tools/commerce-quality-patches/index.html). Use the patch ID as a search keyword to locate the patch.

## Issue

A category merchandising rule cannot combine AND conditions with a condition that matches any of several values.

<u>Steps to reproduce</u>:

1. In the Admin panel, go to **[!UICONTROL Stores]** > **[!UICONTROL Attributes]** > **[!UICONTROL Product]**, click **[!UICONTROL Add New Attribute]**, and create three product attributes with **[!UICONTROL Scope]** set to *Global*: *Category Group* (**[!UICONTROL Dropdown]** with the option *Gold*), *Product Token* (**[!UICONTROL Dropdown]** with the options *Bangle* and *Finger Ring*), and *Occasion Rule* (**[!UICONTROL Text Field]**).
1. Go to **[!UICONTROL Stores]** > **[!UICONTROL Attributes]** > **[!UICONTROL Attribute Set]** and add the three attributes to the *Default* attribute set.
1. Go to **[!UICONTROL Stores]** > **[!UICONTROL Settings]** > **[!UICONTROL Configuration]** > **[!UICONTROL Catalog]** > **[!UICONTROL Visual Merchandiser]** > **[!UICONTROL General Options]**, add *Category Group*, *Product Token*, and *Occasion Rule* to **[!UICONTROL Visible Attributes for Category Rules]**, and click **[!UICONTROL Save Config]**.
1. Go to **[!UICONTROL Catalog]** > **[!UICONTROL Products]** and create six simple products in the *Default* attribute set with these values:

   ```
   SKU      | Category Group | Product Token | Occasion Rule
   simple1  | Gold           | Bangle        | CASUAL WEAR,PARTY WEAR
   simple2  | Gold           | Bangle        | PARTY WEAR
   simple3  | Gold           | Finger Ring   | FOR PARTY
   simple4  | Gold           | Finger Ring   | SUITABLE FOR CASUAL
   simple5  | Gold           | Finger Ring   | THIS IS TEST
   simple6  | Gold           | Bangle        | DAY PARTY
   ```

1. Go to **[!UICONTROL Catalog]** > **[!UICONTROL Categories]**, add a new category, and click **[!UICONTROL Save]**.
1. In the saved category, expand **[!UICONTROL Products in Category]**, set **[!UICONTROL Match products by rule]** to *Yes*, and use **[!UICONTROL Add Condition]** to add these conditions:

   ```
   Attribute       | Operator | Value         | Logic
   Category Group  | Equal    | Gold          | AND
   Product Token   | Equal    | Finger Ring   | AND
   Occasion Rule   | Contains | casual, party |
   ```

1. Click **[!UICONTROL Save]** and check the products assigned to the category.

<u>Expected results</u>:

`simple3` and `simple4`, the *Gold* *Finger Ring* products whose *Occasion Rule* contains *casual* or *party*, are assigned to the category.

<u>Actual results</u>:

No products are assigned to the category, because the **[!UICONTROL Contains]** condition matches *casual, party* as one literal value instead of matching either term.
