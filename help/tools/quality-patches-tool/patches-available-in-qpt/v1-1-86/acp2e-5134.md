# ACP2E-5134: Removing bundled product from Shared Catalog leaves bundle in cart and causes errors at checkout

The ACP2E-5134 patch fixes the issue where removing a product included in a bundle from a company's Shared Catalog leaves the bundle product in the shopping cart, causing an error in the cart and during checkout. This patch is available when the [[!DNL Quality Patches Tool (QPT)]](/help/tools/quality-patches-tool/quality-patches-tool-to-self-serve-quality-patches.md) 1.1.86 is installed. The patch ID is ACP2E-5134. Please note that this issue is scheduled to be fixed in Adobe Commerce 2.4.10.

## Affected products and versions

**The patch is created for Adobe Commerce version:**

* Adobe Commerce (all deployment methods) 2.4.7-p10

**Compatible with Adobe Commerce versions:**

* Adobe Commerce (all deployment methods) 2.4.6 - 2.4.9

>[!NOTE]
>
>The patch might become applicable to other versions with new [!DNL Quality Patches Tool] releases. To check if the patch is compatible with your Adobe Commerce version, update the `magento/quality-patches` package to the latest version and check the compatibility on the [[!DNL Quality Patches Tool]: Search for patches page](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/tools/commerce-quality-patches/index.html). Use the patch ID as a search keyword to locate the patch.

## Issue

Removing a product included in a bundle from a company's Shared Catalog leaves the bundle in the cart and causes cart and checkout errors.
 
<u>Prerequisites</u>:

Adobe Commerce with the [!DNL B2B] extension, with Company and Shared Catalog enabled.

<u>Steps to reproduce</u>:

1. In the Admin panel, go to **[!UICONTROL Stores]** > **[!UICONTROL Settings]** > **[!UICONTROL Configuration]** > **[!UICONTROL General]** > **[!UICONTROL B2B Features]**. Set **[!UICONTROL Enable Company]** and **[!UICONTROL Enable Shared Catalog]** to **[!UICONTROL Yes]**, then click **[!UICONTROL Save Config]**.
1. Go to **[!UICONTROL Catalog]** > **[!UICONTROL Products]** and create three simple products (`simple1`, `simple2`, and `simple3`) and a bundle product (`bundle1`). Add three options to the bundle, one for each simple product. Set **[!UICONTROL Dynamic Price]** to **[!UICONTROL Yes]**, and clear the **[!UICONTROL Required]** checkbox for each option.
1. Go to **[!UICONTROL Catalog]** > **[!UICONTROL Shared Catalogs]** and click **[!UICONTROL Add Shared Catalog]**. Create a **[!UICONTROL Custom]** shared catalog named `Shared Catalog A`, then use **[!UICONTROL Set Pricing and Structure]** to add `bundle1`, `simple1`, `simple2`, and `simple3`.
1. Go to **[!UICONTROL Customers]** > **[!UICONTROL Companies]** and click **[!UICONTROL Add New Company]**. Create a company with **[!UICONTROL Status]** set to **[!UICONTROL Active]** and a **[!UICONTROL Company Admin]**. In **[!UICONTROL Advanced Settings]**, set **[!UICONTROL Customer Group]** to `Shared Catalog A`.
1. Sign in to the storefront as the company admin. Open `bundle1`, click **[!UICONTROL Customize and Add to Cart]**, select all three options, and click **[!UICONTROL Add to Cart]**.
1. In the Admin panel, open `simple2` and expand **[!UICONTROL Product In Shared Catalogs]**. Remove `Shared Catalog A` from **[!UICONTROL Shared Catalogs]**, then click **[!UICONTROL Save]**.
1. On the storefront, sign in as the same customer and open the shopping cart.
1. Open `bundle1`, select `simple1` and `simple3`, and click **[!UICONTROL Add to Cart]**.
 

<u>Expected results</u>:
 
1. The shopping cart displays `Some of the products below do not have all the required options.` The bundle added before `simple2` was removed displays `The required options you selected are not available.` **[!UICONTROL Proceed to Checkout]** remains hidden until that item is removed.
1. `bundle1` with `simple1` and `simple3` is added to the cart as a new line, and no error page appears.

<u>Actual results</u>:
 
Opening the shopping cart displays an error page, and **[!UICONTROL Add to Cart]** fails, preventing the customer from proceeding to checkout. The `var/log/exception.log` file records the warning `Undefined array key "sequence-3"`.
