# ACP2E-5245: New customer uses same address for shipping and billing on first order sees address listed twice at checkout

The ACP2E-5245 patch fixes the issue where a new customer who uses the same address for shipping and billing on their first order sees that address listed twice at the next checkout. This patch is available when the [[!DNL Quality Patches Tool (QPT)]](/help/tools/quality-patches-tool/quality-patches-tool-to-self-serve-quality-patches.md) 1.1.86 is installed. The patch ID is ACP2E-5245. Please note that this issue is scheduled to be fixed in Adobe Commerce 2.4.10.

## Affected products and versions

**The patch is created for Adobe Commerce version:**

* Adobe Commerce (all deployment methods) 2.4.8-p5

**Compatible with Adobe Commerce versions:**

* Adobe Commerce (all deployment methods) 2.4.8 - 2.4.8-p5

>[!NOTE]
>
>The patch might become applicable to other versions with new [!DNL Quality Patches Tool] releases. To check if the patch is compatible with your Adobe Commerce version, update the `magento/quality-patches` package to the latest version and check the compatibility on the [[!DNL Quality Patches Tool]: Search for patches page](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/tools/commerce-quality-patches/index.html). Use the patch ID as a search keyword to locate the patch.

## Issue

Issue where a new customer who uses the same address for shipping and billing on their first order sees that address listed twice at the next checkout.

<u>Steps to reproduce</u>:

1. On the Storefront, click **[!UICONTROL Create an Account]** and register a new customer without adding an address.
1. Add a product to the cart and click **[!UICONTROL Proceed to Checkout]**.
1. In the **[!UICONTROL Shipping]** step, enter a new shipping address, select a shipping method, and click **[!UICONTROL Next]**.
1. In the **[!UICONTROL Review & Payments]** step, select a payment method, keep **[!UICONTROL My billing and shipping address are the same]** selected, and click **[!UICONTROL Place Order]**.
1. Add a product to the cart again and click **[!UICONTROL Proceed to Checkout]**.
1. In the **[!UICONTROL Shipping]** step, check the addresses listed under **[!UICONTROL Shipping Address]**.

<u>Expected results</u>:

The address from the first order is saved once as the default billing and shipping address of the customer, and it's listed once under **[!UICONTROL Shipping Address]**.

<u>Actual results</u>:

The address from the first order is saved twice, once as the default shipping address and once as the default billing address, and it's listed twice under **[!UICONTROL Shipping Address]**.
